# UGC Ad Script Lab

From one product brief, get 10 hook-scored, shoot-ready UGC ad scripts for TikTok, Reels and Shorts — hook, pain, demo beat, CTA — ranked by hook strength, with phone-filming notes.

## When to use
Use when the user wants UGC-style video ad scripts, TikTok/Reels/Shorts ad hooks, creator briefs, or ad angles for a product or service.

## Inputs to collect
- Product, price, offer
- Target customer and their main pain
- Proof: reviews, results, guarantees
- Platform and length (15/30/45 s)
- Claims that are NOT allowed

If something essential is missing, ask for it in one short message (max 3 questions). If it is not essential, state your assumption and continue.

## Workflow
1. **Angle map** — list 10 distinct angles (pain, objection, comparison, unboxing, day-in-life, myth-bust, testimonial, problem-solution, 'I was wrong', founder story).
2. **Write 10 scripts** — one per angle: Hook (0–3 s), Pain (3–8 s), Demo beat (show, not tell), Proof, CTA. Include spoken lines and on-screen text.
3. **Score hooks** 1–10 on curiosity, specificity, pattern-interrupt, relevance. Rank scripts by hook score.
4. **Filming notes** — shot list for a phone: framing, location, lighting, b-roll, props, number of takes.
5. **Compliance check** — remove unprovable claims (health, income, 'guaranteed'); flag platform policy risks.

## Rules
- No fake testimonials presented as real — label them 'script for a real customer to say in their own words'.
- Every claim must trace back to proof the user gave.
- Keep spoken lines conversational; a 30 s script is ~75 words.
- Be specific and actionable; no filler.
- Separate what is verified from what is assumed.

## Output format
Always answer in exactly this structure:

## Ranking
| Rank | Angle | Hook | Hook score |
## Script 1 — <angle> (30 s)
| Time | Visual | Spoken | On-screen text |
